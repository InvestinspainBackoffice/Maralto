# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Belgian and Dutch-speaking property buyers already familiar with INVESTINSPAIN.BE (IIS), browsing specific new-build projects on Spain's Costa del Sol. Warm leads who know the company and are evaluating whether Maralto fits their investment or lifestyle goals. Typical profile: affluent professionals or retirees considering a second home, holiday property, or investment apartment in southern Spain.

## Product Purpose
A standalone landing page for the Maralto residential development in Estepona, designed for direct sharing and targeted distribution to interested prospects. The page exists to convert warm interest into a concrete inquiry: the visitor requests project information (prices, floor plans, availability) via a contact form. It operates independently from the main IIS WordPress site, giving the agent a focused, high-quality link to share with prospects.

## Positioning
IIS positions itself as the Belgian specialist for new-build property on Spain's Costa del Sol. The differentiator is hands-on, Dutch-speaking guidance through the entire buying process — from discovery through inspection trips (100% reimbursed) to purchase and rental management. Maralto itself is positioned as a modern resort-style residence in Estepona combining sea-and-mountain panoramas, premium amenities, and proximity to beach and marina life.

## Operating Context
The page is shared by agent Gunther De Vleeschouwer (real estate associate at IIS) directly with warm leads — via email, WhatsApp, or social messaging. It may also be used as a destination for targeted social media posts. Visitors arrive already knowing IIS exists; they need compelling project detail and a frictionless way to request more info. The page must work without any dependency on the IIS WordPress infrastructure.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Standalone static or lightweight page — no WordPress, no CMS dependency
- Must include a lead-capture form (name, phone, email) with privacy consent
- Dutch language (Flemish market)
- Must render well on mobile (WhatsApp shares dominate)
- Project imagery must come from the developer's renders (user to supply)
- No pricing table on the page — prices and availability are sent post-inquiry
- Starting price "Vanaf €460.000" can be shown as a headline anchor

## Product Principles
1. **Trust over persuasion** — warm leads need confirmation, not convincing; the page should feel authoritative and reliable rather than salesy
2. **Show the life, not just the building** — the imagery and copy should evoke the lifestyle (sea, sun, terraces, resort living) alongside the architecture
3. **One clear action** — every element on the page funnels toward requesting project information; no competing CTAs or navigation away
4. **Mobile-first sharing** — the page will most often be opened from a WhatsApp or email link on a phone; it must be fast and beautiful on small screens
5. **Agent as the human anchor** — Gunther's presence (photo, direct contact) adds personal trust to a digital touchpoint
