The project

4 unique residences,
right on the beach

Atelier 4 by Ikkil Bay is an exclusive project of just 4 unique residences, directly on one of the most iconic beaches of the Costa del Sol in Estepona, close to the marina and the town centre. Each home — Mare, Natura, Stella and Ventu — is designed as an individual villa-like residence with large glass surfaces, natural materials and panoramic sea views.
