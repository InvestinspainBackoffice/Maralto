The project

22 boutique homes
on the New Golden Mile

Solaia is a small-scale project of just 22 modern apartments and penthouses with 2, 3 and 4 bedrooms, located in the quiet and sought-after Cancelada on the New Golden Mile between Estepona and Marbella, surrounded by nature and a stone's throw from beaches, golf, restaurants and local amenities.

Home types

From garden apartment
to penthouse with solarium

The project offers an exclusive living experience with high-quality, eco-conscious homes designed for a Mediterranean lifestyle. The layouts range from spacious apartments with large terraces and garden apartments to pool-view units and luxury penthouses with solarium and panoramic views over the surroundings and the Mediterranean.

Wellbeing & calm

Spa, sauna
& co-working lounge

Solaia stands out with its focus on wellbeing and calm in a boutique setting: beautifully landscaped gardens, a heated outdoor pool, a wellness and spa area with sauna and steam room, a gym/yoga studio and a co-working/lounge space for residents.
