La Cala de Mijas is a vibrant coastal village on the Costa del Sol, ideally situated between Málaga and Marbella. The beach, marina and numerous restaurants are within walking distance. Several golf courses are just a short drive away. Málaga airport is approximately 30 minutes away and Marbella around 25 minutes.
