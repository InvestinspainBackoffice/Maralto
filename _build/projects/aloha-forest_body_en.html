The project

Modern apartments

Aloha Forest offers apartments, penthouses and duplex penthouses with 2 and 3 bedrooms, located in the heart of Nueva Andalucía, close to Marbella's famous Golden Mile. The project is built in two phases: Phase I comprises 16 elegant low-rise units, with construction of Phase II starting in 2026.

The homes

Floor-to-ceiling windows
& a private pool for penthouses

The interior exudes elegance and style, with large floor-to-ceiling windows that let in plenty of natural light and create a smooth transition between indoors and outdoors. Ground-floor apartments come with gardens that can be customised, first-floor apartments have large terraces, and penthouses feature spacious roof terraces with a private pool and panoramic views.

Amenities

Gated community
& concierge services

The gated community offers privacy, security and comfort, with amenities such as swimming pools, concierge services and green spaces. The complex is designed with careful attention to architecture and detail, and every home comes with a parking space in the garage and a storage room.
