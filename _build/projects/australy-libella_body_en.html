Australy Libella is a modern residential development with 67 homes featuring 2 and 3 bedrooms in Selwo, one of the greenest and most tranquil neighbourhoods in Estepona. The project offers a wide range of typologies: high-rise apartments with generous terraces, duplexes with panoramic sea views, penthouses with private solarium and ground-floor units with private garden.

Depending on the type, some homes also include a private pool or jacuzzi. Every home comes with a garage space and private storage. The location near Selwo Adventure and the motorway to Marbella and Málaga makes Australy Libella an excellent choice for both permanent living and holiday use.

Location

Selwo, the green lung
of Estepona

Selwo is one of the quietest and greenest neighbourhoods in Estepona, surrounded by nature yet excellently connected. Estepona's beach is approximately 5 minutes away. The town centre with restaurants, shops and all amenities is just 10 minutes away. Marbella and Puerto Banús are reachable in around 20 minutes, and Málaga airport in approximately 50 minutes.
