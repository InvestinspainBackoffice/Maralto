Architecture & setting

Jardines de las Lagunas is designed with its surroundings in mind: the architecture is elegant and contemporary, embedded in a green setting with lush gardens that enhance the quality of life. The apartments are spacious and light-filled, with terraces as an extension of the living space and views over the gardens and the nature of Mijas.

Private parking and storage are included as standard — a practical advantage that simplifies daily life. Proximity to golf, the beach and the vibrant coastal villages of Fuengirola and La Cala makes this project attractive for both residential use and rental investment.

Jardines de las Lagunas is situated in the coastal zone, a short distance from beaches and golf courses, and within easy driving distance of Fuengirola, Marbella and Málaga airport.
