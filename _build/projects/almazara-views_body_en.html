Istán · Sierra de las Nieves

Townhouses in nature with panoramic views

Almazara Views is an exclusive project of 23 townhouses with 3 bedrooms, located in the charming mountain village of Istán, on the slopes of the Sierra de las Nieves. At the foot of La Concha — the iconic peak above Marbella — the project offers breathtaking panoramic views over the Mediterranean Sea and the surrounding mountain ridges. Adjacent to the project is the Sierra Blanca Country Club Resort, with tennis, golf and dining within walking distance.

The townhouses are designed in a timeless style that blends harmoniously into the landscape. Communal gardens with native plants strengthen the connection with nature, while the communal pool forms the perfect centrepiece for relaxation.

Location

Istán, the green heart of the Sierra de las Nieves

Istán is an authentic Andalusian mountain village in the Sierra de las Nieves, a quarter of an hour from Marbella and Puerto Banús. The village is surrounded by the Sierra de las Nieves National Park and offers an absolute tranquillity that is rare this close to the Costa del Sol. The beaches of Marbella are reachable in less than 20 minutes.
