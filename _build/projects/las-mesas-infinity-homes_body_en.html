The project

Panoramic sea views
to Gibraltar & Africa

Las Mesas Infinity Homes is a boutique development in Estepona with apartments and penthouses of 2 and 3 bedrooms. From an elevated position, the project offers breathtaking views stretching from the Rock of Gibraltar to the African coastline. Every home is designed for a seamless living experience that is both practical and stylish.

The homes

Floor-to-ceiling windows
& seamless terraces

The homes feature floor-to-ceiling windows, motorised blinds, LED lighting and porcelain floor tiles that extend onto the terrace. Every bedroom has an en-suite bathroom. The penthouses offer a private solarium with spectacular views.

Amenities

Wellness centre
& EV charging

The complex offers a comprehensive wellness centre with gym, sauna and Turkish bath, a freshwater swimming pool and landscaped communal gardens. The underground car park is equipped with EV charging stations.
