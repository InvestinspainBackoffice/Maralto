Vanian Views is a modern new-build project with 61 stylish apartments and penthouses featuring 2 and 3 bedrooms in Selwo, the exclusive green neighbourhood of Estepona on the Costa del Sol. The striking semi-oval architecture blends indoor and outdoor spaces seamlessly, letting you fully enjoy the Mediterranean light and the surrounding natural landscape.

The homes are finished with top-of-the-line appliances, high-end materials and luxurious details throughout. Each home includes a private garage and storage unit. The Selwo location combines absolute tranquillity and privacy with excellent access to the beach, Estepona town centre and the motorway towards Marbella.

Location

Selwo, nature and tranquillity
on the edge of Estepona

Selwo is the most exclusive and green residential district in Estepona, located on the border with Marbella. The area offers absolute peace and privacy, while Estepona's beach is just 5 minutes away. The town centre with restaurants and shops is 10 minutes away. Marbella and Puerto Banús are approximately 20 minutes away, and Málaga airport around 50 minutes.
