The project

164 apartments
& 18 homes with private pool

Vission Hills is an exclusive gated new-build development on the Costa del Sol, located in Cala de Mijas in a privileged spot with direct access to the coastal road (A-7) and the AP-7 motorway. The project comprises 164 apartments with 1, 2 and 3 bedrooms spread over six modern buildings (Vission Hills I), complemented by Vission Hills II and III. In addition, the development includes 18 luxury semi-detached homes with 4 bedrooms, each with its own private garden and private pool.

Energy & finishes

Energy rating A,
up to 89% savings

All homes are built with a reinforced concrete structure and pvc or aluminium exterior carpentry with double glazing, achieving energy rating A — good for an estimated energy saving of up to 89% and annual financial savings of up to € 1,100 compared to a rating F home.

Vission Hills III comprises 18 luxury semi-detached homes with 4 bedrooms, each with its own private garden and private pool — for those looking for more privacy and space within the same gated complex.

The communal areas offer a wide range of amenities: landscaped gardens, several swimming pools, a social club, a gym, a coworking space and a spa — all within walking distance of golf courses, shopping centres, supermarkets and restaurants, with the beach less than 2 km away.

Location

Cala de Mijas,
peace, privacy & connectivity

Vission Hills enjoys a strategic location on the Costa del Sol: La Cala de Mijas 2 minutes away, Fuengirola 8 minutes, Marbella 25 minutes and Málaga international airport just 20 minutes. Puerto Banús and the María Zambrano AVE train station in Málaga are also easily reachable.
