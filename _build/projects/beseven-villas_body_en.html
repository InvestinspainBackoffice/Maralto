The project

7 exclusive villas
with private pool and sea views, Marbella

BeSeven Villas is a select collection of seven unique luxury villas in Marbella, near San Pedro de Alcántara. Each villa is an independent masterpiece with its own private pool, spacious terraces and panoramic sea views. The project combines contemporary architectural design with the highest standards of finish and absolute privacy.

The villas

Modern design

The BeSeven villas are designed to put light, space and nature at the centre. Large glass facades, open living rooms and multiple terraces on different levels create a living experience where inside and outside become one. Each villa features multiple bedrooms, each with its own en-suite bathroom.
