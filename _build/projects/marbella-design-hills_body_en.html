The project

A landmark project on
the Golden Mile

Marbella Design Hills is a gigantic project of 92 villa-apartments and a shopping center, developed in collaboration with Dolce & Gabbana. An 80,000 square metre plot has been reserved for this development, on one of the best locations in Marbella: Las Lomas de Marbella Club, on the edge of the Golden Mile.

The residence

Designed by
Dolce & Gabbana

Every space carries the unmistakable signature of the Italian fashion house — from the Mediterranean majolica tiles to the refined materials in the communal areas. A mixed-use project that will become not only a new hub for residential real estate, but also a public space of extraordinary beauty.

The view

From the roof gardens and terraces, enjoy breathtaking sea views, with the hills of Marbella as a backdrop. A strong focus on quality design makes Marbella Design Hills a hub for refined, contemporary living.

Location
