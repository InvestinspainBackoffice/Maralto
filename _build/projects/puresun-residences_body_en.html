PureSun Residences is a modern residential project in Manilva with 2 and 3 bedrooms, designed with light, space and sea views in mind. The project comprises 159 units in total, with 50 planned for the first phase. The name reflects the two core values: pure light and pure sun — the sun and panoramic sea views are the absolute highlights of this development.

The apartments feature generous terraces with unobstructed views over the Mediterranean Sea, from sunrise to sunset. Residents can choose from three personalised design collections — Earth, Sea and Air — allowing them to tailor the finish entirely to their own taste. The communal facilities elevate the living experience to resort level.

Location

Manilva, on the border of
Estepona and Gibraltar

Manilva sits at the western end of the Costa del Sol, between Estepona and the Strait of Gibraltar. The town combines an authentic Andalusian character with an excellent location: beaches, golf courses and the lively La Duquesa marina are within walking distance or just a few minutes away. Estepona is approximately 15 minutes away, and Málaga airport around 70 minutes.
