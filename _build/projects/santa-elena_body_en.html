The project

Modern apartments
in the heart of Fuengirola

Santa Elena is a new residential complex in the heart of Fuengirola, a lively town on the Costa del Sol that's growing increasingly popular among both Spaniards and international residents. The 7-kilometre-long promenade is the place for beach walks, international restaurants and music festivals — a perfect mix of relaxation and entertainment.

The homes

Silestone worktops
& aerothermal hot water

The architecture is modern and clean-lined, with high-quality materials and careful finishing at its core. Large windows let in plenty of natural light, with excellent thermal and acoustic insulation, full air conditioning and a kitchen with quality appliances and a Silestone Compac quartz worktop. Sustainable hot water is generated via an aerothermal system, and buyers can further upgrade their home to their taste.

Amenities

Santa Elena is more than just a place to live: the complex is designed to offer residents an active, social and comfortable lifestyle, with a stylish entrance hall, a coworking space and gym, and a rooftop with a 40 m² pool and a 180 m² sun terrace with sea and mountain views.

Location

Fuengirola,
everything within easy reach

Santa Elena's location is ideal for those who want everything within easy reach: direct access to the A-7 and AP-7 motorways, just 750 metres from the centre of Fuengirola, 1 km from the marina and 1.6 km from the beach. Nearby you'll find schools, supermarkets, sports centres, a shopping centre, the zoo and a water park — ideal for young families!
