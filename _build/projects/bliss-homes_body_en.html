Bliss Homes is a modern residential project of 134 apartments and penthouses spread across 9 blocks in Casares. The project offers a west-southwest orientation for maximum natural light and stunning views of the sea and mountains.

The homes

Gardens, terraces
& panoramic views

Ground-floor apartments feature private gardens, while upper floors offer spacious terraces. Penthouses have large rooftop terraces with expansive views. All homes are fitted with oversized windows for maximum daylight and views of the sea or mountains.

Amenities

Pool, gym
& near golf

The complex offers a communal pool, a fitness centre and landscaped gardens. With 166 underground parking spaces and 134 storage units, there is space for everyone. Several golf courses, including Casares Costa Golf and Finca Cortesín, are less than 15 minutes away.
