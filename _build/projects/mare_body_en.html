The homes

Open living spaces
with large terraces and sea views

The MARE apartments are generously proportioned, with open living areas that flow seamlessly onto the terraces with sea views. Fully fitted kitchens, quality bathrooms and premium finishes define the atmosphere. The apartments are ideal as a primary residence or as an investment on the Costa del Sol.

Location

San Pedro de Alcántara,
between Marbella and Estepona

MARE is located in San Pedro de Alcántara, a popular coastal town between Marbella and Estepona. Puerto Banús with its marina, restaurants and shops is just 5 minutes away. San Pedro's beaches are within walking distance. Málaga Airport is approximately 50 minutes away.
