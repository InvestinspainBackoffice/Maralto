Ultra-luxury villas with Lamborghini design

Tierra Viva is an exceptional new development of 53 exclusive villas in Benahavís, designed in collaboration with Automobili Lamborghini. The Italian sports car brand's bold architectural language has been translated into a living experience unparalleled anywhere else in the world: sharp horizontal lines, dramatic recesses, intense use of colour and a fusion of indoor and outdoor space that pushes every boundary.

The project is nestled in the hills of Benahavís, surrounded by the golf courses of the Golden Triangle and with panoramic views over the Mediterranean. A secured gated community with 24/7 surveillance guarantees absolute privacy and discretion.

The homes

Architecture as an art form

Every villa at Tierra Viva is an architectural statement. The Lamborghini signature translates into powerful geometric volumes, contrasting materials and an aggressively elegant aesthetic that overwhelms both inside and out. The open living spaces flow seamlessly into the outdoor areas, the private infinity pool and the Mediterranean gardens.

The project offers two villa types — Zafiro and Esmeralda — each with its own character and layout, but both with the same uncompromising finish and the same spectacular panorama over the hills of Benahavís and the Mediterranean Sea.

Location

Benahavís, the most exclusive municipality on the Costa del Sol

Benahavís is known as Spain's most exclusive municipality, set in the hills behind Marbella and surrounded by more than 25 golf courses. The village combines absolute peace and privacy with excellent access to Marbella, Puerto Banús and Málaga international airport. Tierra Viva sits centrally in the famous Golden Triangle between Benahavís, Marbella and Estepona.
