19 exclusive villas in a Balinese gated community

One Bali Villas is an exceptional project of 19 individual villas in a secured estate in La Cala de Mijas, on the Costa del Sol. The concept combines Balinese architectural inspiration with the Mediterranean climate: organic shapes, lush greenery and a tranquil atmosphere take centre stage. Each of the 19 villas has a private pool, its own garden and generous terraces.

The complex offers amenities you would normally only find in a boutique resort: a private cinema, a spa with sauna and hammam, and a communal pool with gardens. The gated community guarantees 24/7 security and absolute privacy.

Location

La Cala de Mijas, heart of the Costa del Sol

La Cala de Mijas is a charming coastal village on the Costa del Sol, set between Marbella and Fuengirola. The village combines an authentic Andalusian atmosphere with excellent access to beaches, golf courses and international connections. Málaga airport is approximately 25 minutes away, making this an excellent choice as a holiday home or investment property.
