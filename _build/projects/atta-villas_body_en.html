Location

San Pedro de Alcántara,
between Marbella and Estepona

ATTA Villas is located in San Pedro de Alcántara, a popular coastal town between Marbella and Estepona. Puerto Banús with its marina and restaurants is 5 minutes away. San Pedro's beaches are a short drive away. Málaga Airport is approximately 50 minutes.
