with sea views in Monte Mayor, Benahavís

Vitae Villas Monte Mayor is a select collection of luxury new-build villas located in the prestigious gated estate of Monte Mayor in Benahavís — one of the most exclusive addresses on the Costa del Sol. Each villa benefits from the breathtaking panoramic sea views that characterise Monte Mayor, with far-reaching vistas over the Strait of Gibraltar and the Moroccan coast.

The villas are designed in collaboration with renowned architects and interior designers, combining a contemporary aesthetic with the intimate atmosphere of Mediterranean mountain nature. Private pools, large terraces and carefully landscaped gardens make each of these homes an exceptional private domain.

Location

The estate combines absolute privacy with perfect accessibility: Benahavís village is a few minutes away, Marbella and Puerto Banús are approximately 20 minutes, and Málaga airport is approximately 60 minutes.
