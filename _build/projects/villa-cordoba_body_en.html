Villa Córdoba is an exclusive new-build project of villas in authentic Andalusian style, set in a charming and tranquil residential area at the Mijas Golf course. The architectural design is divided into open, well-connected spaces, with four spacious bedrooms and three or four bathrooms — inspired by a modern and stylish design that breathes the essence of the Mediterranean lifestyle.

Large windows maximise natural light and the connection with the surrounding landscape. A luxurious private swimming pool makes this villa a coveted property for those who love sunshine, nature and Mediterranean living comfort. The project also features aerothermal energy and electric vehicle charging in the garage.

Villa Córdoba combines the timeless beauty of Andalusian architecture with the benefits of modern building standards. Location

Mijas Golf,
overlooking Mijas Pueblo and the Costa del Sol
