Location

Málaga,
close to beach & AVE station

Octavia is strategically located in Málaga, 10 minutes from the AVE high-speed station María Zambrano and Pablo Ruiz Picasso Airport. The AP-7 motorway provides easy access to Marbella and the rest of the Costa del Sol. The project offers direct access to the beaches and the vibrant city life of Málaga.
