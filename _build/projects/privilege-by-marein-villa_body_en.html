Privilege By Marein is a unique turnkey new-build villa in an absolute prime location in Cortijo Blanco, Marbella. This contemporary residence sits directly on the San Pedro promenade, just 1.5 km from Puerto Banús. The property represents a rare opportunity to live on the beach, a stone's throw from the most exclusive amenities on the Costa del Sol.

The residence was designed by the renowned architect Ismael Mérida and spread across four thoughtfully conceived levels, seamlessly connected by a private panoramic elevator. The design blends Mediterranean elegance with premium natural materials such as natural stone and warm wood. Floor-to-ceiling glazing floods every room with light and frames spectacular sea views throughout.

Cortijo Blanco is one of the most exclusive coastal neighbourhoods on the Costa del Sol, directly adjacent to San Pedro de Alcántara. The beach promenade connects you on foot to Puerto Banús in just 1.5 km. International schools, renowned golf courses, luxury boutiques and gourmet restaurants are all within easy reach. Málaga airport is approximately 45 minutes away via the motorway.
