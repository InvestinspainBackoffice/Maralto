The project

55 luxury townhouses,
golf & sea views in Casares

Spinto Blu is an exclusive new development of 55 luxury townhouses with 3 bedrooms in Casares del Sol, within walking distance of Azata Golf and a short drive from the world-famous Finca Cortesín resort. Every home offers panoramic views over the golf course, the sea and the surrounding nature. A rare combination of privacy, luxury and nature on the western Costa del Sol.

The townhouses at Spinto Blu are designed for outdoor living at its finest. Large terraces with unobstructed golf and sea views, a generous living area with open kitchen and 3 bedrooms with en-suite bathrooms. Every detail reflects Mediterranean living at the highest level, with quality finishes and sustainable materials.

Amenities

Communal pool,

Spinto Blu offers a communal pool, landscaped gardens and a private residential atmosphere. The project is located next to Azata Golf and minutes from Finca Cortesín — one of Europe's top golf resorts. Beaches, Estepona and Marbella are easily accessible via the A-7.

Location

Casares del Sol,
Azata Golf & Finca Cortesín

Casares del Sol is located on the western Costa del Sol, between Estepona and Sotogrande. The area is known for its tranquility, nature and proximity to top golf courses such as Azata Golf and Finca Cortesín. Estepona is approximately 15 minutes away, Marbella 30 minutes.
