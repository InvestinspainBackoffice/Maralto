Las Mesas Sea Suites is an exclusive new-build project located in the heart of Estepona, on the sunny Costa del Sol. This modern apartment complex combines comfort, style and a sustainable lifestyle. With generous terraces, a wellness area and a saltwater pool, outdoor living blends seamlessly with the interior.

The homes

From garden apartment
to penthouse with roof terrace

The complex consists of apartments with two or three bedrooms, ranging from garden apartments to penthouses with roof terraces. The homes are designed with plenty of natural light and offer views over the park and the sea. Open-plan living areas, high-end Bosch kitchen appliances and modern bathrooms create a comfortable and elegant living environment.

Amenities

Gym, Turkish bath
& EV charging points

The setting is quiet and exclusive, with secure access and a wide range of amenities such as a gym, Turkish bath, sauna and underground parking with electric vehicle charging points. The project has been built with sustainability in mind, featuring solar panels, water-saving installations and an advanced climate control system.
