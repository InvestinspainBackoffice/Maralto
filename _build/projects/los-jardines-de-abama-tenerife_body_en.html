Location

Guía de Isora,
the sunniest part of Tenerife

Los Jardines de Abama is located in Guía de Isora on the south-west coast of Tenerife — the island's sunniest zone. Abama Resort is 20 minutes from Tenerife South Airport and 45 minutes from Santa Cruz de Tenerife. Tenerife enjoys a pleasant climate year-round.
