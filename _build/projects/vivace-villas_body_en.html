The project

30 villas in
Valle Romano

Nestled within the green surroundings of Valle Romano, Vivace Villas offer an extraordinary blend of modern living and nature. An exclusive collection of 30 villas where modern elegance meets the tranquillity of a golf course.

The residence

Three typologies,
each uniquely designed

Vivace Villas are designed in three typologies — Arpeggio, Vibrato and Legato — with 3 or 4 bedrooms. Each villa is uniquely designed to fit its individual plot, with contemporary architecture that maximises the views and guarantees privacy.

The view

Mountain and sea views

Each villa combines contemporary architecture with the natural landscape, making the most of the views over the mountains and the sea.

Amenities

Passive architecture,
sustainably designed

Every villa features a private garden and swimming pool, underfloor heating and en-suite bathrooms. Built according to the principles of passive architecture, with sustainable materials and energy efficiency.
