The project

Helvet Green is a stylish apartment project of 103 homes with 1 to 3 bedrooms in Mijas, east of Marbella. The project is designed for tranquility, space and connection with nature: Mediterranean gardens, a natural pool and relaxation areas give Helvet Green its unique character. Ideal for those who combine nature with coastal living.

The apartments at Helvet Green are designed for maximum light and space. Large windows, open living areas and spacious terraces create a sense of freedom.
