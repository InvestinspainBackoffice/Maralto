The project

40 homes,
frontline Estepona Golf

Attire Estepona is a new-build project of 40 semi-detached homes with 3 bedrooms, located in a quiet residential setting bordering Estepona's golf course. The homes are designed for those who truly want to live on the Costa del Sol: a spacious open-plan living area on the ground floor, a luxurious master bedroom suite with walk-in wardrobe and bathroom, and two parking spaces and storage for every home.

Location

Quiet, yet
excellently connected

The location in Estepona's western expansion zone is both quiet and excellently connected: Puerto Banús is about 25 minutes away, Marbella and Gibraltar 30 minutes, and Málaga Airport 50 minutes. Shops, schools, hospitals and Estepona beach are just a few minutes away.
