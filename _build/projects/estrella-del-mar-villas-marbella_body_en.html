The project

Modern villas
with sea views and private garden in Elviria, Marbella

Estrella del Mar Villas is an exclusive villa development in Elviria, the green and peaceful east of Marbella. The villas combine contemporary architecture with quality finishes and generous outdoor spaces. Private pool, garden and sea views are included as standard, and the beaches and golf courses of Elviria are within walking distance.

The villas

Light-filled living areas
with seamless garden access

The Estrella del Mar villas feature spacious open living rooms that open fully onto the terrace and garden. Kitchens are fully fitted and each bedroom has its own en-suite bathroom. Premium materials and a clean, contemporary design create a comfortable and stylish living environment.

Location

Elviria,
the green east of Marbella

Estrella del Mar Villas is situated in Elviria, one of the most sought-after neighbourhoods in East Marbella. The area is known for its pine forests, wide beaches and the Elviria Golf Club. Marbella town centre is 15 minutes away, Málaga Airport 30 minutes.
