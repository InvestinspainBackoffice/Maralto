Location

Ojén,
above Marbella in nature

Palo Alto is located in Ojén, a picturesque mountain village just 10 minutes from Marbella town centre. The resort borders the Sierra de las Nieves national park. Marbella's beaches, Puerto Banús and the golf courses of the Golden Mile are all within easy reach. Málaga Airport is approximately 45 minutes away.
