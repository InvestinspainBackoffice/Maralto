Elie Saab Villas is an exceptional collection of just 5 prestigious villas in Sierra Blanca, Marbella's most exclusive neighbourhood. Each villa spans approximately 1,000 m² across three levels on a plot of around 2,200 m². The collaboration with the world-renowned fashion house Elie Saab translates into interiors of unparalleled elegance: flowing lines, premium eco-friendly materials and a refined colour palette.

The villas face south with panoramic views over the sea and mountains. Surrounded by pine forests and lush subtropical greenery, they offer absolute privacy in a setting unmatched on the Costa del Sol. Each villa features 4 spacious en-suite bedrooms, a private elevator and an infinity pool.

Location

Sierra Blanca, the pinnacle
of Marbella

Sierra Blanca is Marbella's most exclusive residential district and one of the most prestigious addresses on the Costa del Sol. Surrounded by pine forests on the slopes of the Sierra Blanca, the neighbourhood combines absolute peace and privacy with an excellent location. Marbella's town centre and Puerto Banús are just 10 minutes away. Málaga airport is approximately 45 minutes.
