The project

Wellness apartments
with panoramic views

Kosmos is a new-build project in East Marbella that puts wellbeing at the centre. The apartments and penthouses with 2 and 3 bedrooms are designed with premium materials, smart home technology and large windows that let in abundant natural light. Every apartment features a generous private terrace that flows seamlessly from the living area.

The homes

Open living spaces
with private terraces

The interior combines style and functionality, with open-plan living areas that flow seamlessly outdoors through large sliding doors. Bedrooms are separated for maximum privacy, each with an en-suite bathroom. The penthouses offer spectacular rooftop terraces with breathtaking views of the Mediterranean Sea and the surrounding mountains.

Amenities

Pools, spa
& co-working

The communal facilities are designed for a healthy lifestyle: two swimming pools (including a heated one), a spa, a fully equipped gym and a co-working space with an office kitchenette. Everything is designed to promote active wellbeing and relaxation.

Location

East Marbella,
close to sea & motorway

Kosmos is strategically located in East Marbella, with easy access to the AP-7 motorway, beaches and Marbella town centre. The area offers a mix of nature, golf courses and beach clubs. Málaga Airport is a short drive away.
