The project

68 terraced homes,
sea views & La Cala de Mijas

Cala Serena Sun is a gated residential complex of 68 south-facing terraced homes with 3 to 4 bedrooms in La Cala de Mijas, east of Marbella. Each home includes a private garage, spacious terrace and garden, with many offering open sea views. The complex combines comfort, light and the best of Mediterranean outdoor living.

The terraced homes at Cala Serena Sun are south-facing for maximum natural light. Each home has 3 or 4 bedrooms, a spacious terrace, private garden and garage. Location

La Cala de Mijas,
beach & golf nearby

La Cala de Mijas is a charming seaside village on the Costa del Sol, located between Marbella and Fuengirola. The village has its own beach, harbour, restaurants and a lively centre. Several golf courses are a short drive away, and Málaga Airport is approximately 30 minutes.
