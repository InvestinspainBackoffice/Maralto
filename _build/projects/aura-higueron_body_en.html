Aura Higueron is an exclusive new development offering refined penthouses, garden villas with private pools and upscale apartments in the Higuerón neighbourhood, Fuengirola. The homes feature 2 to 3 bedrooms with south-western exposure and innovative terrace construction that maximises outdoor space with sea views.

The homes

Jacuzzi, spa
& innovative terraces

Most units feature a jacuzzi. Ground-floor garden villas have a private pool, while penthouses offer a spacious solarium. Every home includes two parking spaces and a storage room. High-end finishes and modern infrastructure are standard throughout the project.

Amenities

The complex offers two south-facing communal pools, a spa with heated pool and sauna, a co-working space, gym, padel courts and tennis courts. A wellness centre completes the offering.
