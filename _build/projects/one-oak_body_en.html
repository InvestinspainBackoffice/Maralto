The project

Modern living
in a prime Torremolinos location

One Oak is an exclusive residential project in Torremolinos reflecting Mediterranean living at its purest. Here, nature, architecture and comfort come together in a harmonious living environment. Modern lines, large windows and generous terraces bring the outdoors in, with panoramic views over the Torremolinos coastline and the Bay of Málaga.

The homes

From ground-floor home
to penthouse with terrace

One Oak offers apartments with 1 to 4 bedrooms, penthouses and ground-floor homes — each with spacious outdoor areas where indoors and outdoors blend seamlessly. Thanks to the thoughtful orientation of the homes, natural light floods in abundantly, creating warm and sustainable living comfort.

Location

Opposite a school,
next to Pinar del Moro

Set in a quiet, well-connected neighbourhood — opposite Sunny View School and next to the protected Pinar del Moro nature reserve — One Oak puts every convenience within reach. Schools, sports facilities, supermarkets and leisure areas are within walking distance, while the beach, Málaga airport and the vibrant town centre are just minutes away by car.
