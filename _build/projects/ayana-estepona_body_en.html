Ayana Estepona is an exclusive new-build project of luxury apartments situated on the New Golden Mile, on the coastal strip between Estepona and San Pedro de Alcántara. The project stands out for its spacious terraces, high-quality finishes and excellent communal amenities — all set in a green, peaceful environment.

Ayana's architecture breathes a modern Mediterranean atmosphere: open spaces, large windows and terraces that put outdoor living centre stage. A swimming pool, gym and communal gardens form the heart of the complex. The New Golden Mile location guarantees a perfect mix of tranquillity and accessibility.

Design & layout

Open living spaces

Ayana Estepona's apartments are generously and brightly designed, with underfloor heating, high-quality kitchens and terraces that fully integrate outdoor living. The open kitchen-living area flows seamlessly onto the terrace — ideal for Mediterranean living all year round.

The New Golden Mile is one of the most sought-after locations on the Costa del Sol, less than 10 minutes from Estepona town centre and approximately 20 minutes from Puerto Banús. The motorway is close by, but the complex is set in a quiet, green environment within a short distance of the beach.
