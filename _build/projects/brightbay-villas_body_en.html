Location

La Herradura,
authentic Costa Tropical

La Herradura is a quiet, authentic coastal town on the Costa Tropical between Málaga and Granada, known for its sheltered bay, clear sea and the nearby Marina del Este. In the surrounding area you'll find beach restaurants, watersports and the typical Spanish charm of the neighbouring villages.
