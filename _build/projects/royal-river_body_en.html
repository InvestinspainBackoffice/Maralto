The project

16 luxury residences,
spa & Golf Río Real

Royal River is a small-scale luxury project of 16 exceptional residences in the prestigious Golf Río Real urbanisation of Marbella. The offering includes apartments, ground-floor homes with private gardens, and penthouses with terraces up to 200 m². An exclusive address combining quality, tranquility and a prime location.

The homes

Open living area,
private terrace & top-quality finishes

The homes at Royal River combine a bright open living and dining area with a sleek kitchen and a spacious terrace overlooking the sea or golf course. Penthouses feature terraces up to 200 m² — ideal for outdoor living year-round. Finishes and materials are of the very highest quality.

Amenities

Pool, solarium,
spa & fitness room

Royal River offers its residents a comprehensive set of communal facilities: pool with solarium, spa, wellness zone, sauna and fitness room. The complex is located in Golf Río Real, one of the most prestigious golf urbanisations on the Costa del Sol, a short distance from the beach and Marbella town centre.

Location

Golf Río Real,
beach & Marbella centre

Golf Río Real is one of the greenest and most exclusive urbanisations in Marbella, situated between the N-340 and the coastline. Within walking distance of the beach and a few minutes' drive from the charming Marbella town centre and the Golden Mile. Málaga Airport is approximately 40 minutes away.
