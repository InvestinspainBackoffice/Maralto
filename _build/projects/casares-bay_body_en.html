The project

Coastal apartments,
sea views & communal pool

Casares Bay is a modern apartment project in the peaceful surroundings of Casares Costa, near Sotogrande on the western Costa del Sol. The apartments offer sea views, communal pool and a green setting. The location is ideal for those seeking peace and nature, within walking distance of the coast and a short drive from all amenities.
