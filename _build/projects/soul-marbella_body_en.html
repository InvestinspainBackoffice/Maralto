The project

Apartments, penthouses
& villas

Soul Marbella is a new-build project comprising several unique phases, with apartments, penthouses and villas of 2 to 5 bedrooms. At Soul Marbella, every line and angle is carefully designed to achieve a balanced effect, making optimal use of the ever-present sunlight that streams through the glass doors and windows of every room.

The residence

Spacious, light-filled
interiors

The homes feature spacious, light-filled interiors with large windows that make the most of the sunlight. Refined finishes and elegant bathrooms make every home a place to fully enjoy.
