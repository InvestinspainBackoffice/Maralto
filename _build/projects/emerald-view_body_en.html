The project

Villas in Mijas Pueblo,

Emerald View is an exclusive residential project in picturesque Mijas Pueblo, one of the most beautiful white villages on the Costa del Sol. The homes offer spectacular panoramic sea views over the Mediterranean, private pool and an authentic setting. Living in Mijas Pueblo means enjoying peace, nature and beauty — with the coast just a short drive away.
