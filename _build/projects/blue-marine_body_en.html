Manilva is a quiet coastal municipality on the western Costa del Sol, between Estepona and Sotogrande. The area combines authentic charm with easy connections to Sotogrande, Gibraltar and Marbella. Málaga Airport is approximately 80 minutes away, Gibraltar Airport around 30 minutes.
