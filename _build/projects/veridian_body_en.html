The project

Sustainable townhouses
in El Higuerón

Veridian is an exclusive new-build project of semi-detached eco-homes with 3 bedrooms, located in El Higuerón — a green and international residential area on the Costa del Sol. The townhouses combine contemporary design with sustainable principles: solar panels, energy-efficient installations, private gardens and spacious terraces with panoramic views over the Mediterranean and the surrounding greenery.

Location

El Higuerón,
green and international

El Higuerón mainly attracts an international crowd and offers every amenity for comfortable living: the beach is 15 minutes on foot, Carvajal train station 15 minutes, international schools nearby, and Málaga Airport 20 minutes away. Within 10 minutes you'll find restaurants, sports facilities and a Hilton hotel.
