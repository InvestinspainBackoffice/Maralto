Living between

Grand Bay is a new residential project located in Bahía de las Rocas in Manilva, a quiet and up-and-coming region on the Costa del Sol. The development sits between Punta Paloma and the Sotogrande marina, offering a unique combination of nature, sea and open views.

The project comprises apartments and penthouses with 2 and 3 bedrooms, each with 2 bathrooms and spacious terraces. Many homes enjoy beautiful sea views thanks to their favourable orientation. Depending on the type, homes feature a private garden or solarium.
