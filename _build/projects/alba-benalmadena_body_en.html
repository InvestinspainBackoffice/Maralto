The project

Apartments and townhouses

Alba Benalmádena is a modern residential project in Torremuelle, Benalmádena, offering 2 and 3-bedroom apartments and townhouses. Every type of home enjoys spectacular panoramic views over the Mediterranean Sea. The project combines contemporary design with a privileged natural setting.

The homes

Spacious terraces
with unobstructed sea views

Alba Benalmádena's apartments and townhouses are bright and well-planned. The terraces are generous and offer uninterrupted views of the Mediterranean Sea. Every home includes two underground parking spaces and a storage room.

Amenities

Infinity pool, gym
& landscaped gardens with sea views

Alba Benalmádena offers residents an infinity pool with sea views, communal gardens, a gym and a fitness room. The setting in Torremuelle, surrounded by nature and close to the sea, makes this project exceptionally attractive for permanent living and investment alike.
