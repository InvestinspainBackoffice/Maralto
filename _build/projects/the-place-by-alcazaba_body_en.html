The project

Modern living
at Alcazaba Lagoon

The Place by Alcazaba is a contemporary residential development of 38 spacious homes in Casares, near Alcazaba Lagoon. The project emphasises comfort and wellbeing with refined finishes and modern design. Residents enjoy access to Alcazaba Lagoon's facilities, including water sports, restaurants and beach bars.

The homes

Private gardens, solariums
& sea views

Ground-floor apartments feature private gardens and pools. Upper floors offer solariums with sea views and private pools. All homes have spacious south or south-east facing terraces with floor-to-ceiling windows and double glazing.

Amenities

Spa, gym
& water sports

The complex offers a modern spa with treatments, a contemporary sauna, a fitness centre, a shared pool surrounded by landscaped gardens and a solarium. Residents have access to Alcazaba Lagoon for kayaking, paddleboarding and more.
