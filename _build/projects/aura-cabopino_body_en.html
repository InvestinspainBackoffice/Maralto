The project

5 unique villas
inspired by Ibiza

Aura Cabopino comprises 5 unique villas inspired by the golden era of Ibiza, located in Cabopino on Marbella's eastern side. Each villa blends premium materials with panoramic views and open spaces across multiple levels. From the private terrace, you enjoy golf and sea views, within walking distance of Cabopino Golf.
