The project

Three unique villas
on the Golden Mile

Noura comprises three exclusive villas — Moon, Sky and Sun — located on Marbella's Golden Mile, above the renowned Puente Romano. Each plot spans approximately 1,200 m² with built areas of 535 to 557 m². The villas feature abundant natural stone and exceptional light thanks to oversized windows.

The homes

Infinity pool, private lift
& wine cellar

Each villa features an infinity pool, private lift, bio-ethanol fireplace, wine cellar and jacuzzi. Kitchens are fitted with Miele appliances and oak cabinetry. The Sonos audio system and fully integrated smart home technology ensure the ultimate in comfort. Bedrooms offer en-suite bathrooms and an abundance of natural light.

Finishes

The villas are finished with natural stone, oversized windows and premium materials. Every home comes with air conditioning, alarm system, smart home technology and parking or garage.
