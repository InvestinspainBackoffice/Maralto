Azata Delmare is a modern residential complex with 74 apartments and penthouses featuring 2 and 3 bedrooms, strategically located just 250 metres from the beach in Casares Costa. The project comprises 7 residential buildings within a gated community that ensures maximum privacy and security. The predominantly south-facing orientation guarantees brightness and natural light in every home.

The apartments and penthouses are designed to make the most of sea views and the mountain backdrop. The complex features a beautiful infinity pool, a co-working space and a covered fitness area — all within the secure environment of the gated community.

Location

Casares Costa, beach and golf
close to La Duquesa marina

Casares Costa sits at the western end of the Costa del Sol, between Estepona and Manilva. Azata Delmare's location — 250 metres from the beach and close to La Duquesa marina — makes it particularly attractive for beach lovers and golfers. Estepona is approximately 20 minutes away, Marbella around 35 minutes and Málaga airport around 65 minutes.
