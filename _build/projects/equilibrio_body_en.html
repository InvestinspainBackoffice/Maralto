The project

15 elegant apartments,
220m from the beach & sea views

Equilibrio is an intimate boutique project of just 15 elegant apartments spread across three low-rise blocks in Estepona. Only 220 metres from the beach, the project offers sea views, a communal pool and Mediterranean gardens. Underground parking and lift are included. A rare combination of tranquility, comfort and beach proximity.

Location

Estepona,
beach & marina within walking distance

Estepona is one of the most authentic and beloved towns on the Costa del Sol. With flower-lined streets, a charming marina and excellent restaurants, it combines the charm of a Spanish village with modern resort amenities. Marbella is approximately 30 minutes away, Málaga Airport around 75 minutes.
