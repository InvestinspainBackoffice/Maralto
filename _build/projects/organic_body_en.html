Organic is an exclusive boutique project of just 25 homes with 3 and 4 bedrooms, set in the prestigious El Higuerón district of Fuengirola. Thanks to its low building density, the project enjoys a quiet and private living environment with panoramic views over the Mediterranean Sea. As the name suggests, nature, tranquillity and quality of life take centre stage.

The architecture of Organic is inspired by the natural surroundings and follows the contours of the hillside with elegance. Large windows, open living spaces and generous terraces create a seamless connection between indoors and outdoors. Every home includes a private swimming pool and spacious outdoor areas, perfect for enjoying the Mediterranean climate all year round.

Location

El Higuerón, the most sought-after
district of Fuengirola

El Higuerón is one of the most exclusive and green districts on the Costa del Sol, set on the hills above Fuengirola with beautiful sea views. The location combines peace and privacy with excellent connectivity: beaches, sports facilities, wellness, restaurants and shops are all close by. Marbella is approximately 20 minutes away, and Málaga airport around 20 minutes.
