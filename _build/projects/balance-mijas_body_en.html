The project

Comfortable living
with panoramic views

Balance Mijas is a contemporary project of 75 apartments and penthouses with 2 and 3 bedrooms in Las Lagunas de Mijas. From an elevated position, you enjoy panoramic views of the sea and mountains. The project is designed for a balanced lifestyle with spacious outdoor areas and extensive communal facilities.

The homes

Fully fitted kitchens
& spacious terraces

Every home features a fully equipped kitchen with induction hob, fridge, dishwasher, microwave, oven and extractor hood. Terraces reach up to 100 m², and ground-floor apartments come with private gardens. The contemporary design emphasises light, space and views.

Amenities

13,000 m² gardens
& social spaces

The complex offers over 13,000 m² of landscaped gardens, a central pool with chill-out zone, a gym, social club, barbecue facilities and picnic areas. Everything is designed for relaxation, entertainment and wellbeing.

Location

Las Lagunas de Mijas,
