The project

Contemporary villas
in nature

El Bosque en Alcuzcuz is an exclusive project of 8 villas in La Reserva de Alcuzcuz, Benahavís. The estate spans 24,000 m², with individual plots of approximately 3,000 m², surrounded by pine and olive trees. Phase I (4 villas) has been delivered and sold; Phase II (4 villas) is currently under construction.

The homes

Spacious rooms
with abundant light

The villas combine contemporary design with high-quality construction. Large floor-to-ceiling windows provide abundant natural light and offer panoramic views of the mountains and the sea. Each villa features a private garden with pool, garage and a basement for extra space.

Amenities

Gated community
with 24-hour security

The enclosed estate offers complete privacy and tranquillity, with 24-hour security and direct access to nature. The villas are nestled among the trees, with views of the Serrania de Ronda and the Mediterranean Sea.

Location

Benahavís,
at the foot of Serrania de Ronda

El Bosque en Alcuzcuz is set in a serene mountain landscape at the foot of the Serrania de Ronda. Despite its tranquil setting, Estepona, Marbella and Puerto Banús are just a few minutes' drive away. Nearby you'll find top golf courses such as Los Arqueros and La Quinta Golf.
