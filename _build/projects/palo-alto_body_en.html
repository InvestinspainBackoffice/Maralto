The project

A hilltop community
on 50 hectares

Palo Alto is an exclusive hilltop community in the mountains of Ojén, just minutes from Marbella, with beautiful uninterrupted views over the Mediterranean Sea. The estate spans 50 hectares, of which only 20% will be built — so you always remain surrounded by a green, natural environment.

The residence

State-of-the-art design
by Villarroel Torrico

Villarroel Torrico, the architects behind Palo Alto, have stood for excellence and aesthetic perfection for 40 years at a regional, national and international level. Every apartment has a spacious terrace with beautiful views of the Mediterranean Sea and very generous interior surfaces, designed around the hills of the Sierra de las Nieves.
