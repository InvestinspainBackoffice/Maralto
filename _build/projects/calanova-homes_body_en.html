The project

Golf villas on the fairway,
sea views & private pool

Calanova Homes is a luxury villa project directly on the Calanova Golf Club in Mijas Costa. The villas are positioned to offer direct access to the fairway while also providing panoramic sea views over the Mediterranean. An exceptional combination of golf, nature and luxury living — in one of the finest locations on the Costa del Sol.

The villa

Open living area,
modern kitchen & fairway view terrace

The Calanova Homes villas are spacious and light-filled, with large windows that draw in the golf course and sea views. The open living and dining room opens onto a spacious covered terrace with private pool. The modern kitchen and luxurious bathrooms are finished with premium materials. Each villa has its own garage and storage room.

Location

Calanova Golf,
Mijas Costa & beach minutes away

Calanova Golf is located in Mijas Costa, in the heart of the Costa del Sol between Fuengirola and Marbella. La Cala de Mijas and the beach are just minutes away by car. The A-7 offers fast connections to Málaga Airport (35 min) and Marbella (20 min).
