Mijas · Costa del Sol

108 apartments in 6 buildings, surrounded by nature

Bahía is a substantial new build project of 108 apartments spread across 6 buildings in Mijas, on the Costa del Sol. With 2 or 3 bedrooms per apartment and carefully designed communal grounds, Bahía offers a complete living environment for families, couples and investors. The gated community guarantees security and tranquillity.

The location in Mijas combines the best of both worlds: close to the coast and the beaches of La Cala de Mijas, yet also near the hills and the picturesque village centre of Mijas Pueblo. All everyday amenities are easily accessible.

The homes

Bright apartments with generous terraces

The apartments at Bahía are designed with a focus on light, space and practical comfort. The open kitchen-living area leads to generous terraces that invite outdoor living. With 2 or 3 bedrooms, the homes are ideal for families or as a spacious holiday home. Finishes are quality and modern throughout.

The 6 buildings are grouped around shared outdoor spaces, creating a sense of community and belonging. The gated community provides 24/7 access control and a safe environment for children and residents.

Location

Mijas, between coast and hills

Mijas is one of the most popular municipalities on the Costa del Sol, with both a coastal zone (Mijas Costa) and a picturesque hilltop village (Mijas Pueblo). Bahía's location offers easy access to the beaches of La Cala de Mijas and Fuengirola, several golf courses and the motorway towards Marbella and Málaga. Málaga airport is approximately 25 minutes away by car.
