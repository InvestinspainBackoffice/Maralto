The architecture

Cascading terraces,
abundant natural light

The homes feature cascading terrace design and are characterized by abundant natural light, spacious open living areas and generous terraces with panoramic views of the Mediterranean Sea. On clear days, the view extends to Gibraltar and the African coast. Interior and exterior flow seamlessly together thanks to ceiling-high windows.

Quality & Sustainability

Premium finishes,
smart technology

Interiors are finished to the highest standards with fully integrated kitchens, premium ceramic tiles and thermally insulated windows. An aerothermal system, solar panels, ducted air conditioning, mechanical ventilation and smart home technology provide modern comfort and sustainability.
