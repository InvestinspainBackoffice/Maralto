Vitta Marina is a modern and functional residential complex with 72 apartments featuring 1, 2 and 3 bedrooms in La Cala de Mijas. All homes have a south or south-west orientation, maximising Mediterranean light throughout the day. Many units enjoy sea views. La Cala de Mijas beach is just a 2-minute walk away.

Ground-floor apartments feature a private garden, while penthouses boast generous terraces with panoramic sea views. Every home includes at least one parking space and private storage. The communal areas are designed for relaxation, sport and outdoor living in a green setting close to the sea.

Location

La Cala de Mijas is a vibrant coastal town on the Costa del Sol, known for its authentic character and excellent location between Málaga and Marbella. The beach, marina and numerous restaurants are within walking distance of Vitta Marina. Several golf courses are just a short drive away. Málaga airport is approximately 30 minutes away and Marbella around 25 minutes.
