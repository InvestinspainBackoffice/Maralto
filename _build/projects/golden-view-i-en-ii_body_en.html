Manilva is an authentic coastal municipality on the western tip of the Costa del Sol, bordering Sotogrande and approximately 30 minutes from Gibraltar. The municipality has its own beach, marinas and a peaceful village centre. It offers an excellent alternative for those who want to avoid the bustle of Marbella but still benefit from a Costa del Sol location.
