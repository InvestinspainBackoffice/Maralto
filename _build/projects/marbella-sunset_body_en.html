Layout & terraces

Marbella Sunset's apartments are designed with an open floor plan that maximises sea views. Large sliding doors connect the living room seamlessly to the terrace — generous enough for a seating area, an outdoor dining space and a sun lounge zone. The penthouses feature an additional rooftop terrace with 360° views.

The bathrooms are finished in marble with high-quality fittings. The kitchens are equipped with top appliances. The project is close to the dune beach of Cabopino and the marina of the same name — one of the most beautiful spots along the entire Costa del Sol.
