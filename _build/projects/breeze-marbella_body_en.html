Breeze Marbella is an exclusive residential project of 34 apartments and penthouses with 2, 3 and 4 bedrooms, set in a privileged elevated position above the Guadalmina Golf course in San Pedro de Alcántara. Every home enjoys wide panoramic views over the golf course, the sea and the surrounding hills of the Costa del Sol.

The project features a co-working space and fitness room, enabling residents to stay productive and active without leaving their tranquil living environment. Breeze Marbella combines the serenity of the golf zone with the proximity of all amenities in San Pedro and Marbella.

Location

San Pedro de Alcántara,

San Pedro de Alcántara is a lively town west of Marbella, known for its wide promenade, bustling market and excellent restaurants. Breeze Marbella is set above the Guadalmina Golf course, a short distance from San Pedro town centre and the beach. Marbella Golden Mile is approximately 15 minutes away, Puerto Banús approximately 10 minutes.
