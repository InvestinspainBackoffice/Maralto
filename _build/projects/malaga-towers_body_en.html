Iconic twin towers
with rooftop spa and infinity pool in Málaga

Málaga Towers is an impressive project of two iconic high-rise towers that will dominate the skyline of Málaga. The towers offer luxury apartments with panoramic views over the city and the Mediterranean Sea, combined with an exceptional range of communal amenities on the upper floors.

The facilities include a rooftop infinity pool with sea views, a spa, gym, co-working spaces and a children's play area — all in the heart of Andalusia's most vibrant city. Málaga is booming: with a growing cultural offering, international top restaurants and excellent connectivity, it has become one of the most dynamic destinations in Europe.

Amenities & lifestyle

Hotel-like services
on the upper floors of both towers

Málaga Towers stands out for its impressive package of communal amenities. The rooftop infinity pool offers a spectacular view over Málaga and the sea. The private spa, indoor gym and outdoor fitness zone are available daily for residents. Co-working spaces with meeting rooms make working from home more enjoyable than ever.

The apartments themselves are sleekly and modernly finished, with large windows that maximise the panoramic views. Málaga city centre is within walking distance: the marina, the historic old town and the airport are all effortlessly accessible.
