La Finca de Jasmine – The Villas is an exclusive project of new-build villas in Benahavís, one of the most sought-after locations between Marbella and San Pedro de Alcántara. The villas are designed with a modern Mediterranean architecture that makes optimal use of the surroundings — with open spaces, large windows and seamless transitions between inside and outside.

Each villa offers a private swimming pool, a generous garden and terraces overlooking the surrounding hills and the Mediterranean Sea. Benahavís is known as the finest gastronomic municipality on the Costa del Sol and offers a haven of tranquillity just minutes from the vibrant centre of Marbella.

Location

Benahavís,
tranquillity and nature on the Costa del Sol

Benahavís is an authentic Andalusian mountain village internationally renowned as the gastronomic capital of the Costa del Sol. The municipality borders Marbella and San Pedro de Alcántara and offers a unique combination of tranquillity, nature and luxury. Golf courses, beaches and the vibrant centre of Marbella are all within easy reach.
