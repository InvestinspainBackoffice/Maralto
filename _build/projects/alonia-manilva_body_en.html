The project

60 modern homes
with views over the golf course

Alonia Manilva is an innovative project of 60 multi-family homes: ground-floor apartments, duplexes and penthouses with 2 or 3 bedrooms, each with its own garage and storage room in the basement. The variety of housing types means there's something for everyone, all with spacious terraces and panoramic views over the golf course.

Location

Easy to reach,
close to La Duquesa Marina

Alonia Manilva enjoys a privileged location with a direct connection to the main roads along the Costa del Sol and Campo de Gibraltar. Nearby you'll find La Duquesa Marina, a popular meeting point for international tourism with an extensive range of local and international dining, and a prestigious frontline golf course.

Comfort & leisure

Green areas, pool
& gastro lounge

Every home stands out for its spacious terrace — an outdoor space ideal for relaxing and enjoying the panoramic view over the golf course. The communal areas are designed for comfort and luxury: landscaped green zones, a pool with a relaxing sun-lounger beach area, and a gastro lounge that becomes the perfect place to socialize.
