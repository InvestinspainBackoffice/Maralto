The project

60 apartments,

Almina Residences is a contemporary new-build project located in Estepona, where modern architecture and a high-quality living experience come together in a green setting. This project comprises 60 stylish apartments and penthouses with 2 and 3 bedrooms, designed with a focus on comfort, light and quality of life.

The residence

Spacious apartments,

The homes are spacious and bright, with large windows and generous terraces that provide a seamless transition between interior and exterior. Depending on the type, you enjoy extra features such as a private garden on the ground floor or a solarium with panoramic views for duplexes and penthouses. Floor heating, ceiling-high windows and fully equipped kitchens ensure comfort.
