The home

Open living spaces
with spectacular views

The villas feature spacious, open living areas with large glass facades that seamlessly bring the outdoors in. Every detail is considered: premium finishes, modern kitchens and en-suite bedrooms. The terraces offer unobstructed views across the Mediterranean Sea and the coastline.

Amenities

Each villa has its own private pool and sun terrace. The Higuerón Golf Club is within walking distance, as is the Fuengirola seafront promenade. Málaga Airport is less than 20 minutes away.
