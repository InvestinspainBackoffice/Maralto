The project

Hillside villas in Marbella,

Serenity Hills Marbella is an exclusive residential project on the green hills of Marbella with panoramic sea views over the Mediterranean. The villas and apartments combine contemporary architecture with a serene, green setting. Peace, space and luxury are central — at one of the finest hilltop locations on the Costa del Sol.
