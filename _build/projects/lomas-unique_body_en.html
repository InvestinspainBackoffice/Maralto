Lomas Unique is an exclusive residential concept at the renowned El Higuerón resort in Fuengirola that combines luxury with functionality in a unique setting. The project comprises three distinct elements: the Unique Garden Villas, the Unique Apartments and the Unique Sky Solarium Penthouses. Each offers a unique living experience while sharing world-class amenities.

Every home comes with a private garage and storage unit and access to the communal swimming pool. The lush green surroundings and controlled access guarantee privacy and security. El Higuerón resort is renowned for its exceptional location, hotel and sports facilities and breathtaking sea views across the Costa del Sol.

Location

El Higuerón, Fuengirola

El Higuerón is one of the most exclusive residential areas on the Costa del Sol, set on the hills above Fuengirola with a spectacular view over the Mediterranean. The Higuerón Hotel and its amenities are within walking distance. Málaga airport is approximately 25 minutes away, Marbella approximately 35 minutes and the beach approximately 5 minutes.
