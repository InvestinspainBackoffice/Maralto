The project

Lomas Unique is an exclusive residential project within El Higuerón Resort in Fuengirola. It comprises garden villas, apartments and sky solarium penthouses, each with private terraces and sea views. Residents enjoy all resort facilities, including the CURIO by HILTON hotel and Michelin-starred restaurants.
