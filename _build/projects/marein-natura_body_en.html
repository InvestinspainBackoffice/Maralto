Exclusive new-build villas
in the golf valley of Nueva Andalucía

Marein Natura is a boutique project of exclusive villas in the golf valley of Nueva Andalucía, just minutes from Puerto Banús and the centre of Marbella. The project features two distinct villa types — Thyma and Laurus — each with its own character, but united by the same high construction and finishing quality.

The villas are designed with a contemporary architecture that blends seamlessly with the green surroundings of the golf valley. Generous terraces, private swimming pools and carefully landscaped gardens make each of these homes a private oasis. Nueva Andalucía is the preferred location for those who want to be close to the vibrance of Marbella while enjoying privacy and tranquillity.

The Thyma type is characterised by a sleek contemporary façade with pronounced horizontal lines and generous terraces. The Laurus villas offer a more panoramic layout with large glass façades facing the garden and pool. Both types are finished with materials of the highest quality: from natural stone and wood to top kitchen appliances and home automation.

The location in the heart of the golf valley of Nueva Andalucía guarantees privacy and tranquillity, while Puerto Banús and the many golf courses of the Golf Valley are just a few minutes away.

Location

Nueva Andalucía,
heart of the Marbella golf valley

Nueva Andalucía is one of the most exclusive residential neighbourhoods of Marbella, situated in the famous Golf Valley — a green valley surrounded by some ten golf courses. It is the preferred destination for golf enthusiasts and those who combine privacy with perfect accessibility. Puerto Banús is approximately 5 minutes away, Marbella town centre 10 minutes, and Málaga airport approximately 55 minutes.
