Character villas next to El Chaparral Golf

Soleia Living El Chaparral is a project of 69 contemporary villas in Andalusian style, located directly next to El Chaparral Golf & Country Club in La Cala de Mijas. Each villa has 3 or 4 bedrooms, a private pool and a spacious private garden — perfect for those seeking space, privacy and a vibrant setting on the Costa del Sol.

The project is south-facing, designed for maximum sun exposure and sea views. The Andalusian architectural style gives the complex a warm, Mediterranean character that blends beautifully with the rolling landscape around the golf course.

Location

La Cala de Mijas, nature and beach on the Costa del Sol

La Cala de Mijas is one of the most sought-after coastal villages on the Costa del Sol. It combines a relaxed atmosphere with an excellent location: walking distance to the beach, directly next to El Chaparral Golf and a 15-minute drive from Fuengirola and Marbella. Málaga international airport is easily reached via the A-7.
