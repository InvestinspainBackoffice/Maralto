La Quinta de Cerrado is an exclusive project of new-build villas situated on a gated estate in La Cala de Mijas, on the hills above the Costa del Sol. Each villa features a private pool, a generous garden and terraces with views of the sea and the surrounding golf courses. The architecture combines modern lines with the warm materials of the Mediterranean building tradition.

Location

La Cala de Mijas,
green village with beach and golf on the Costa del Sol

La Cala de Mijas is a charming coastal village in the hills of Mijas, with its own beach, golf courses and a pleasant village centre. It offers an authentic atmosphere less commonly found in the more touristy towns. Fuengirola is 10 minutes away, Marbella 30 minutes, and Málaga airport approximately 25 minutes.
