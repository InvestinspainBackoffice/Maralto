Zenith Estepona is a contemporary residential project of 40 exclusive apartments and homes with 1, 2 and 3 bedrooms, centrally located in the vibrant centre of Estepona. The combination of prime location, modern architecture and high-quality finishes makes Zenith one of the most attractive investment projects in this segment of the Costa del Sol.

The project is housed within a gated community, guaranteeing maximum security and privacy. The homes feature generous terraces and are surrounded by communal areas with carefully designed outdoor spaces. Estepona's famous flower promenade, marina and beaches are within walking distance.

Location

Estepona town centre,
the Garden of the Costa del Sol

Estepona is one of the most authentic and vibrant coastal towns on the Costa del Sol. The historic centre, marina and famous flower promenade are within walking distance of Zenith. The A-7 motorway provides easy connections to Marbella (± 25 min), Gibraltar (± 40 min) and Málaga airport (± 60 min). An ideal base for enjoying the full coastal life of the Costa del Sol.
