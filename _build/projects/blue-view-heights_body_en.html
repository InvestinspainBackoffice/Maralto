Location

Manilva,
where sea, mountain and peace meet

Manilva is a tranquil coastal municipality at the western end of the Costa del Sol, on the border with Cádiz. Its setting offers spectacular views over the Strait of Gibraltar, the Moroccan coast and the Rock of Gibraltar. Estepona is approximately 15 minutes away, Sotogrande and the region's excellent golf courses approximately 15 minutes, Gibraltar approximately 20 minutes.
