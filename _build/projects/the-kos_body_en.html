The homes

Townhouses and sky top villas
with private terrace and solarium

The KOS townhouses and sky top villas feature spacious private terraces and solariums with sea views. The interiors are modern and thoughtfully designed, with attention to sustainable materials and energy-efficient solutions. Air conditioning, double glazing and high ceilings are standard throughout.

Amenities

Spa, gym, coworking
& 3 infinity pools

The KOS offers its residents an impressive range of amenities: 3 shared infinity pools, a heated indoor pool, spa, gym, coworking zone and landscaped gardens. Golf courses are nearby. A community for those who combine excellence with conscious living.

Location

Benalmádena,
Costa del Sol with golf courses and nature

The KOS is in Benalmádena, on the Costa del Sol, surrounded by nature and close to golf courses and beaches. Fuengirola is 10 minutes away, Málaga city centre and the airport approximately 25 minutes. A peaceful, green location with all the conveniences of the Costa del Sol on your doorstep.
