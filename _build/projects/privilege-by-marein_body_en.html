The architecture

Designed by
Ismael Mérida

The residence is designed by renowned architect Ismael Mérida and spread across four thoughtfully designed levels, seamlessly connected by a private panoramic lift. The design combines Mediterranean elegance with premium natural materials such as natural stone and warm wood. Floor-to-ceiling windows fill the entire home with an abundance of natural light and spectacular sea views.

Comfort & entertainment

Cinema room, gym
& rooftop solarium with second pool

The main level features a generous open lounge and dining area with a bio-ethanol fireplace, connected to an Italian designer kitchen with top-of-the-range appliances. Large glass sliding doors open onto the covered terrace, the garden and the heated infinity pool. The basement level includes a covered heated pool, a fully equipped gym, a private cinema room and a relaxation area. The panoramic rooftop terrace features a second pool, an outdoor kitchen and a lounge with sweeping sea views.
