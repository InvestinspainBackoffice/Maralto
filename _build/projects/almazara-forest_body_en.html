The project

Living in nature,
near Marbella

Almazara Forest is an exclusive new-build project in Istán, on the edge of the Sierra de las Nieves nature reserve and just 10 minutes from Marbella. The modern 2 to 3 bedroom apartments are surrounded by native vegetation and offer a unique combination of tranquillity, nature and proximity to the coast.

The homes

Panoramic sliding doors,
large porcelain floors

The apartments are characterised by panoramic sliding glass doors that seamlessly connect indoor and outdoor living. Large porcelain floors, Balay kitchen appliances and LED lighting create a modern and comfortable living environment. Each home includes two underground parking spaces.

Amenities

Pool, gardens
& sustainable technology

The complex features a shared pool, landscaped gardens with native plants, underground parking with EV charging points and digital connectivity. Energy class A is achieved through LED lighting, air conditioning and thoughtful design.
