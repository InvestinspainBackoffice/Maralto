Apartments in harmony with nature

Almazara Forest is located in the beautiful Sierra de las Nieves, just outside Marbella, in the charming mountain village of Istán. The project offers modern apartments with 2 and 3 bedrooms, generous terraces and breathtaking views over the bay of Marbella and the surrounding protected natural park. Contemporary architecture meets warm materials and premium finishes.

The homes achieve class A energy efficiency and feature Balay kitchen appliances, sand-coloured large-format porcelain floors and naturally designed bathrooms. Open living spaces connect to wide terraces with a spectacular panoramic view over the bay of Marbella. A rare combination of nature, tranquillity and proximity to the city.

Location

Istán, nature and silence
within minutes of Marbella

Istán is an authentic mountain village in the Sierra de las Nieves, just 10 minutes from central Marbella. From this green paradise you have a spectacular view over the bay while being completely surrounded by nature. Marbella and its beach are reachable in approximately 10 minutes, Puerto Banús in approximately 15 minutes and Málaga airport in approximately 45 minutes.
