Interior & comfort

Light-filled living spaces
with master suite and open kitchen

Upon entering, you are welcomed into a bright and open living area. The living room, dining area and kitchen flow seamlessly into each other and open onto the terrace and pool, creating a perfect indoor-outdoor connection. The fully integrated kitchen is sleek in design with a direct connection to the dining and seating area.

The master suite is located on the ground floor and features an en-suite bathroom with a freestanding bathtub, double vanity and a generous walk-in wardrobe. On the basement floor there are two additional bedrooms, each with built-in storage and a private shower bathroom — perfect for guests or family.

Wellness & entertainment

Unique basement floor
with sauna, home cinema, bar and billiards

What truly sets Villa Syzygy apart is its fully equipped basement floor. A generous lounge and living space with a seating area, room for a grand piano and a billiard table forms the heart of this level. The atmospheric LED lighting integrated into the ceiling creates a warm and inviting ambience.

The basement also features a stylish bar with seating, a sauna, a home cinema and an additional bathroom with shower. Two storage rooms provide ample space. A garage and additional storage room are included in the price.

Location

Cancelada,
between Estepona and Marbella

Cancelada is a quiet and authentic village on the New Golden Mile, a short distance from both Estepona and San Pedro de Alcántara. The location offers the best of both worlds: the peace and privacy of a residential setting, combined with quick access to the beaches, golf courses, restaurants and shops of the Costa del Sol. Puerto Banús and the marina of Estepona are approximately 20 minutes away.
