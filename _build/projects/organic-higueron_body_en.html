The project

25 homes,
in harmony with nature

Organic is an exclusive boutique project of just 25 homes with 3 and 4 bedrooms, located in the prestigious El Higuerón in Fuengirola. Thanks to its low building density, the project enjoys a quiet, private living environment with panoramic views over the Mediterranean.

Architecture that
follows the terrain

The architecture of Organic is inspired by its natural surroundings and elegantly follows the contours of the hillside. Large windows, open living spaces and generous terraces create a harmonious connection between indoors and outdoors. All homes feature a private pool and spacious outdoor areas.
