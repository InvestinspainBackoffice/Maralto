Frontline apartments with views of La Gomera

Solum is an exclusive new development in Guía de Isora, on the western coast of Tenerife. The location is literally frontline: 50 metres from the beach with unobstructed panoramic views over the Atlantic Ocean and the island of La Gomera. Apartments are available with 1 to 4 bedrooms and interior areas from 115m², complemented by outdoor spaces from 36m².

Solum offers a comprehensive wellness experience: a sauna, Turkish bath, fitness room and wellness zone are all included in the complex. The Guía de Isora area combines the mild climate of Tenerife's west coast with the tranquillity of a still relatively undiscovered region.

Location

Guía de Isora, the peaceful west coast of Tenerife

Guía de Isora is one of the most peaceful and authentic municipalities in Tenerife, located on the west coast with a permanently mild climate. The area is characterised by unspoilt nature, azure waters and a broad sea panorama stretching all the way to the island of La Gomera. Costa Adeje and Tenerife South Airport are easily reached by motorway.
