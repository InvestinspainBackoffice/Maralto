The project

Frontline by the sea on
the New Golden Mile

Sierra Blanca by the Sea is an exceptional and prestigious new-build project, directly on the beach of Estepona, on one of the last available frontline plots on the Costa del Sol. Maximum privacy, luxury and a unique location with panoramic views over the Mediterranean Sea.

The residence

A limited collection,
a complete resort feel

The project consists of a limited collection of just 6 exclusive villas and 42 apartments and penthouses, all designed to make the most of natural light, sea views and the surrounding nature. Elegant, timeless architecture with high-quality materials and spacious terraces that connect indoor and outdoor living harmoniously.

The view

Panoramic sea views,
right on the frontline

Thanks to its frontline location, you look directly out over the Mediterranean Sea — from the terrace, the pool or the villa garden. One of the last plots on the Costa del Sol with this direct connection to the beach.
