The project

Seven modern villas
on the Calahonda coast

Lantana Villas is an exclusive new-build project consisting of seven modern villas, located in beautiful Calahonda, close to the sea. Here, comfort, privacy and high-end finishes come together in a unique living environment. The villas are designed with spacious, bright interiors, large windows and generous terraces, including private pools and solariums.

The villas

Spacious plots,
& generous terraces with solarium

Set on spacious plots, these villas offer 4 to 6 bedrooms and 4 to 5 bathrooms, ideal for families and lovers of luxury. The setting is peaceful yet close to all amenities: schools, shops, leisure and the coast are all within easy reach. With two parking spaces per villa, convenience is complete.
