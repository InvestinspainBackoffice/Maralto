The project

49 luxury homes,
next to the golf course

Las Villas Sotogrande is an exclusive new-build project comprising 49 premium homes with 3 and 4 bedrooms, located in the prestigious Sotogrande Alto in Cádiz, directly next to the La Cañada golf course. The project combines luxury living with an exceptional natural setting where green space and outdoor living take centre stage.

The development comprises several home types, including patio homes, semi-detached homes and detached villas, each with its own character and layout.
