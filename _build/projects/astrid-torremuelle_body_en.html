Astrid Torremuelle is a modern residential project of 74 homes in Benalmádena Costa, one of the most sought-after coastal locations on the Costa del Sol. Location

Benalmádena Costa,
between beach, golf and the city

Benalmádena Costa lies between Fuengirola and Torremolinos, on the beautiful coastal strip of the Costa del Sol. Astrid Torremuelle is situated near the Torremuelle golf course and within walking distance of the beach. The A-7 motorway provides easy access to Málaga airport (± 20 min), Fuengirola (± 10 min) and Marbella (± 35 min). Benalmádena Puerto Marina, the vibrant marina, is approximately 5 minutes away.
