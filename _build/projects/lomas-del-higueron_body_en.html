Lomas del Higuerón is an ambitious residential project designed for those who want to get the most out of Mediterranean living. Every detail has been carefully considered to let residents enjoy their home every day: from the spacious apartments and penthouses with floor-to-ceiling windows to the extensive communal amenities that deliver a genuine resort experience.

The project is situated within a gated community in Higuerón — one of Fuengirola's most exclusive residential areas — with spectacular sea views and direct access to the nature of the Sierra de Mijas. A co-working zone, concierge, spa and gym complete a residential offer that is unique in this segment on the Costa del Sol.
