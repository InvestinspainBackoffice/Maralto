Gardens by the sea

Mãla Kai — Hawaiian for 'gardens by the sea' — is an exclusive new development within walking distance of the beach in Estepona. The complex features contemporary apartments and spacious penthouses with panoramic Mediterranean views, surrounded by lush gardens and green outdoor spaces. An ecological approach runs through the entire project, from architectural choices to materials and energy sources.

The complex is designed as a secured gated community with 24/7 surveillance. Every home enjoys sun-drenched terraces and a refined interior that connects seamlessly with the outdoor environment. The result is a living space where tranquillity, nature and modern architecture come together.

The homes

Space, light and sea views

The apartments and penthouses at Mãla Kai are designed for those who seek the best of both worlds: the space and privacy of a private home, combined with resort-style amenities. Large window walls bring in Mediterranean light and visually connect the interior with the gardens and sea panorama.

The penthouses offer oversized terraces with unobstructed views across the Mediterranean — perfect as a permanent residence or an exclusive holiday retreat on the Costa del Sol.

Location

Estepona, the garden city of the Costa del Sol

Estepona is known as one of the most authentic and green coastal towns on the Costa del Sol. The town invests heavily in flower gardens, promenades and cultural heritage, and consistently scores very high in quality-of-life rankings. Mãla Kai is within walking distance of the beach, while also offering a quick connection to Marbella, Puerto Banús and Málaga international airport.
