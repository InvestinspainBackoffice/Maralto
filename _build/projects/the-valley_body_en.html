The project

23 contemporary homes,
in the hills of Elviria

Welcome to The Valley, an exclusive boutique project made up of just 23 contemporary homes, located in the quiet hills of Elviria in Marbella East. The project is designed with a clear focus on modern architecture, natural light and a strong connection to the surrounding nature.

The residence

Open living spaces,
floor-to-ceiling windows

The homes stand out for their open living spaces, where the living room, dining area and open kitchen flow seamlessly into one another. Large floor-to-ceiling windows provide an abundance of daylight, while premium finishes such as parquet flooring, double glazing and carefully selected materials contribute to a refined and comfortable living experience.

Amenities

Gym, wellness
& children's play area

In many homes, the indoor-outdoor living experience is enhanced by private infinity pools overlooking the valley, the mountains or landscaped gardens. The whole is set within a secure, gated residence with a lift, video intercom and electric-vehicle charging points.
