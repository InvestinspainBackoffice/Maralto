Location

La Alcaidesa,
golf & views of Gibraltar

La Alcaidesa is located on the western Costa del Sol, close to Sotogrande and Gibraltar. The municipality is known for its golf courses and breathtaking views. Gibraltar Airport is approximately 20 minutes away, Málaga Airport around 90 minutes.
