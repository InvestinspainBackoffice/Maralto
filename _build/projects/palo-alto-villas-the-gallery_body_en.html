Palo Alto Villas – The Gallery is an exceptional project of just four ultra-luxury villas, each named after an iconic painter: Van Gogh, Klimt, Monet and Pollock. Located in the exclusive gated resort of Palo Alto in Marbella, these villas combine masterful architecture with the highest possible standards of finishes and materials.

Each villa has a unique character that reflects the spirit of its namesake — but all four share the same exceptional quality, private pool, lush garden and panoramic sea views over the Costa del Sol. Palo Alto gives residents access to the resort's sports and wellness facilities.

The four villas

Van Gogh, Klimt, Monet, Pollock —
each a masterpiece in its own right

The four villas of The Gallery are designed with the same philosophy as the artworks after which they are named: each unique in execution and character, but all of unimpeachable quality. The open living spaces flow seamlessly onto covered terraces and gardens. The interiors are finished with hand-selected materials by renowned architects.

Palo Alto is one of the few resorts in Marbella with an integrated sports and wellness offering, including padel, tennis and a resort club. The hillside location above Marbella guarantees privacy and an unobstructed panoramic sea view.
