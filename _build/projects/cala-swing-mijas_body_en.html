The project

155 apartments,
golf & sea on the doorstep

Cala Swing Mijas is a contemporary residential project in La Cala de Mijas, strategically located next to Calanova Golf Club and a short distance from the sea. The 155 apartments with 1, 2 and 3 bedrooms offer an excellent combination of quality, location and lifestyle. Ground-floor units with private gardens and penthouses with golf views complete the offering.
