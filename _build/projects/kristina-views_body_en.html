Kristina Views is a boutique development of just 20 townhouses in Manilva, between Estepona and Sotogrande. The homes combine natural materials with modern architecture and offer open living spaces, fully fitted kitchens and views of the green surroundings and the sea.

The homes

Private gardens

Each townhouse features a private garden with the option for its own pool, terraces overlooking the green hinterland and the sea, and a rooftop solarium. Corner units offer an extra bedroom and bathroom. The homes come with air conditioning, carport and fully finished interiors.

Location

Manilva,
between Estepona & Sotogrande

Kristina Views is located in Manilva, just 3 minutes from La Chullera beach and 4 minutes from La Duquesa marina. Nearby you'll find top golf courses such as Finca Cortesín and Real Club Valderrama. Estepona town centre is 20 minutes away.
