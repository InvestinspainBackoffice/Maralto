The Oak 48 is an exclusive residential project of 48 homes in a privileged location near the centre of Estepona. The project stands out for its contemporary architecture, combining design and functionality in a setting with breathtaking views over the Mediterranean and the mountains of the Costa del Sol.

The homes are designed to make the most of the Mediterranean climate: generous outdoor spaces, a solarium and private pool create an authentic luxury living experience. The project is aimed at those who appreciate a contemporary lifestyle in one of the most dynamic coastal towns on the Costa del Sol.

The Oak 48 embraces an architectural vision where every detail is carefully attuned to its surroundings. Location

Estepona,
the Garden of the Costa del Sol

Estepona is known as the "Garden of the Costa del Sol" — an authentic, vibrant coastal town with a beautiful marina, flower promenade, historic centre and countless restaurants. The Oak 48 is strategically located near the town centre, with easy connections to the beach, motorway and the rest of the Costa del Sol. Marbella is approximately 25 minutes away, Gibraltar approximately 40 minutes.
