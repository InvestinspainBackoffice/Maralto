The project

Eight villas in
the hills of Nueva Andalucía

In the tranquil hills of Nueva Andalucía, Marbella, rises Ocho de Oro — a landmark project that transcends the definition of luxury living. A curated collection of eight villas with exclusive interior design by Versace Home, where Mediterranean beauty meets the unmistakable codes of one of the world's most iconic fashion houses.

The residence

Each residence is a celebration of Versace's unparalleled design heritage, where architecture, fine Italian materials and craftsmanship converge in a statement of refined elegance. From the last mosaic tile to the smallest door handle — everything bears the signature of Versace Home.
