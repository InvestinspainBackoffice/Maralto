Cerrado Hills is an exclusive development of 13 individual high-end villas, each with 4 bedrooms, set on the hills of Mijas on the Costa del Sol. Every villa offers panoramic views over the surrounding golf courses and the Mediterranean Sea — a combination that sets these homes apart from all other available properties in the region.

The construction and finishing quality is exceptionally high, with materials and execution that far exceed the standards of comparable projects on the Costa del Sol. Cerrado Hills is located just 20 minutes from Málaga airport, in an excellently accessible setting with everything nearby.

Each villa at Cerrado Hills features 4 bedrooms, of which 3 are on the upper floor and 1 adjoins the living area. The generous basement offers extensive possibilities: additional bedrooms, a games room, a fitness room, a home cinema, storage space, or any combination of the above — making each villa fully adaptable to the owner's personal needs.

The fitness and sports facilities are excellent — ideal for those wishing to stay active alongside their rounds on the surrounding golf courses. The elevated position guarantees privacy and views, while Fuengirola and the beach are just a short drive away.

Location

Mijas,

Mijas is a municipality on the hills above the Costa del Sol, encompassing both the idyllic white village of Mijas Pueblo and the vibrant coastal strip of Mijas Costa. Cerrado Hills enjoys an elevated position with panoramic views, close to golf courses, the beach of Fuengirola and Málaga international airport. Marbella is approximately 25 minutes away.
