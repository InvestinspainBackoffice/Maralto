Location

Las Lagunas de Mijas,
between Fuengirola and La Cala

Rayos del Sol is located in Las Lagunas de Mijas, a centrally placed coastal area between Fuengirola and La Cala de Mijas. Beaches are a short drive away, and several golf courses lie in the immediate vicinity. Marbella is 20 minutes away, Málaga Airport 25 minutes.
