The project

17 luxury villas &
110 villa-apartments

Marbella Club & Puente Romano Hotels (Marbella Club Group) know exactly what their guests need — and translate that into Marbella Club Hills: 17 luxury villas and 110 villa-apartments, penthouses and duplex-apartments with 3 or 4 bedrooms, in the same style, elegance and luxury as the iconic hotels. Located in Benahavís, at the foot of Marbella Club Golf.

The residence

Refined architecture,
sea and mountain views

The elegant architecture is designed to appeal to the refined eye, with large floor-to-ceiling windows and patio doors that flood the interiors with natural light and open onto beautiful private terraces. The view of the Mediterranean Sea always takes centre stage.
