The project

44 exclusive residences,
penthouse with private pool & sea views

Ocean View Marbella is a gated community of 44 exclusive residences and 10 penthouses with private pool, located in the hills of Marbella bordering a protected nature reserve. The complex offers a heated communal pool, concierge service, 24-hour security and panoramic sea views. Luxury living in a green, peaceful setting, close to everything Marbella has to offer.

The residences

2 & 3 bedrooms,
penthouses & panoramic views

Ocean View Marbella offers 2-bedroom apartments (127–132 m²), 3-bedroom apartments (149–177 m²) and 3-bedroom penthouses (133–195 m²) with private pool. Every home enjoys spacious terraces with sea views, high-quality finishes and the comfort of a full-service residence.

Amenities

Heated pool,

The complex features a heated communal pool, sun terrace, covered outdoor kitchen, concierge service and 24-hour security. Underground parking and private storage are included. Situated in the green hills of Marbella, a short distance from golf clubs, beaches and the town centre.

Location

Ocean View Marbella is located in the hills east of Marbella, bordering a protected nature reserve. A short distance from several golf clubs, the beaches of the Costa del Sol and Marbella's historic town centre. Málaga Airport is approximately 40 minutes away.
