Vangård is an exclusive new-build project located in Fuengirola, designed for those seeking a combination of modern architecture, premium finishes and an exceptional lifestyle on the Costa del Sol. The project comprises elegant apartments and penthouses with 2, 3 and 4 bedrooms, designed with spacious living areas, large terraces and abundant natural light. The open layouts create a seamless transition between indoor and outdoor living. Various types feature generous terraces, private gardens or impressive solariums with panoramic views.
