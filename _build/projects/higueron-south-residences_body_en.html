Higuerón South Residences is an avant-garde residential project with 164 homes at the Higuerón Hotel resort in Fuengirola. The design is inspired by the renowned Higuerón Hotel and offers a unique combination of exclusive living and professional hotel services. The homes — with 2 and 3 bedrooms — are open and spacious, with large sliding doors that maximise natural light.

The complex comprises 70 Deluxe Apartments, 49 Garden Villas, 25 Sky Villas and 20 Sky Penthouses, surrounded by native vegetation. Every home enjoys spectacular sea views and direct access to the Higuerón Hotel resort's amenities, including world-class restaurants, wellness and sports facilities.

Higuerón South Residences offers four distinct home types for every lifestyle. The Deluxe Apartments (70 units) are open and light-filled, with panoramic sliding doors. The Garden Villas (49 units) provide direct access to lush gardens. The Sky Villas (25 units) combine space and elevation with breathtaking sea views. And the Sky Penthouses (20 units) are the absolute pinnacle — spectacular duplex residences with private views over the Mediterranean.

The avant-garde design is inspired by the Higuerón Hotel, with attention to architectural line, natural light and connection with nature. All homes are surrounded by native vegetation and offer direct access to the resort's hotel and wellness facilities.
