The project

164 homes,
four artistic blocks

Higuerón South Residences is an ambitious residential project of 164 homes in Fuengirola's Higuerón area. It comprises 70 Deluxe Apartments, 49 Garden Villas, 25 Sky Villas and 20 Sky Penthouses, spread across four blocks named after artists: Leiro, Brinkmann, Darko and Alfil. Architecture and art go hand in hand here.

The homes

Private terraces, pools
& sea views

Each home features spacious private terraces with sea views. The Garden Villas and Sky Villas have their own pools. The architecture combines Corten steel, stone and wood in a design that blends seamlessly into the natural surroundings. Art installations throughout the complex give it a unique character.

Amenities

Heated pools,
spa & wellness

The complex offers heated indoor and outdoor pools, gym, spa, wellness, sauna, solarium and padel and tennis courts. The landscaped gardens with native vegetation and art installations create an atmosphere of luxury and serenity.

Location

Higuerón,
by the sea in Fuengirola

Higuerón South Residences is located in Fuengirola's Higuerón area, close to the beach and the train station. Excellent connections and proximity to Málaga Airport at approximately 20 minutes make this the ideal location for those seeking the best of the Costa del Sol.
