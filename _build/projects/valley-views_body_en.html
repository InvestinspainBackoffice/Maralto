The project

Contemporary living,

Valley Views is a contemporary new-build project in Mijas Costa that combines modern architecture with breathtaking valley views. The 2 to 3 bedroom apartments offer open-plan living, elegant kitchens and spacious terraces where you can fully enjoy the Andalusian light and landscape.

The homes

Open-plan living,
elegant kitchens

The apartments are designed with an open-plan concept that maximises light and space. Elegant kitchens, modern bathrooms and large windows form the foundation. Each apartment features a spacious terrace with panoramic views over the green valley.

Amenities

Large pool,
landscaped gardens & EV charging

Valley Views offers a large communal pool with solarium, landscaped gardens, underground parking with EV charging points, lift and security system. The complex features aerothermic heating and solar panels for optimal sustainability with Energy Label A.
