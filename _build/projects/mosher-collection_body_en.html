The project

44 residences,
inspired by Robert Mosher

Mosher Collection is a limited luxury project of 44 homes — ground-floor apartments and penthouses with 1 to 3 bedrooms — in the prestigious Rancho Domingo urbanisation in Benalmádena. From an elevated position, the residences enjoy panoramic sea views and are inspired by the timeless architectural philosophy of American architect Robert Mosher: harmonious forms, attention to the surroundings and a strong bond with the landscape.
