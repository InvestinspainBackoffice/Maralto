The project

Apartments with panoramic
sea and mountain views in Benahavís

Altura 160 is an exclusive new-build project with modern apartments and penthouses featuring 2 and 3 bedrooms, located in the sought-after Benahavís — between Marbella and Estepona. The residence sits on an elevated position, allowing almost all homes to enjoy impressive panoramic views of the Mediterranean Sea, coastline and surrounding mountains.
