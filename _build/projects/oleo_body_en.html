The project

Modern villas,
private pool & sea views

Oleo is a contemporary villa project in Mijas with stylish architecture, private pool and sea views. The villas combine space, light and high-quality finishes with an idyllic setting on the Costa del Sol. Every design is focused on maximum comfort and enjoying the Mediterranean climate — from the pool, the terraces or the living room.

The interior

Open living area,
terrace & modern kitchen

The Oleo villas feature an open living area with large windows and direct access to the outdoor terrace with private pool. The modern kitchen is fully equipped. Spacious bedrooms with en-suite bathrooms and built-in wardrobes provide every comfort. Sustainable materials and refined finishes characterise every detail.
