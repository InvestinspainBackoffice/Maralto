The project

Airy apartments,
sea views & New Golden Mile

Aire is a modern apartment project in Cancelada, located on the New Golden Mile between Estepona and Marbella. The name reflects the light, airy atmosphere the project exudes: open architecture, spacious terraces and sea views create a feeling of freedom and space. An ideal choice for those who want to enjoy the best of the Costa del Sol.

The apartments

Spacious terrace,
open living area & modern interior

The apartments at Aire feature spacious terraces with sea views, a modern open kitchen and bright living spaces. The finishes combine quality materials with a contemporary design. Every home is designed with outdoor living at its heart — from the sunset terrace to the communal gardens.

Amenities

Aire offers a communal pool, landscaped gardens and shared communal spaces. Cancelada on the New Golden Mile is a quiet neighbourhood within walking distance of shops and restaurants. The beach, golf and the A-7 motorway are reachable within minutes.
