Location

Guía de Isora,
west coast of Tenerife

Las Villas del Tenis is located at the Abama Resort in Guía de Isora, on the sunniest side of Tenerife. Los Gigantes is 10 minutes away, Tenerife South Airport approximately 45 minutes. The resort has its own private beach and is surrounded by spectacular natural scenery.
