The project

Modern apartments

Calanova Collection is a contemporary apartment development directly adjacent to the prestigious Calanova Golf Club in La Cala de Mijas. The apartments enjoy sea views, generous terraces and premium finishes. With a gym, communal pool and exceptional location, this project offers a complete lifestyle on the Costa del Sol.

Location

La Cala de Mijas,
between Marbella and Fuengirola

Calanova Collection is located in La Cala de Mijas, a peaceful coastal village between Marbella and Fuengirola. The area features several top golf courses and La Cala's beaches are a short drive away. Marbella is 20 minutes away, Málaga Airport 30 minutes.
