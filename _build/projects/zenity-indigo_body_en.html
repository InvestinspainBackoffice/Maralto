33 spacious homes with terraces up to 120m²

Zenity Indigo is a carefully composed new development of 33 luxury homes in Estepona, close to the beach on the New Golden Mile. The project includes apartments, penthouses and duplexes with 2, 3 or 4 bedrooms, and features terraces of up to 120m² — a rare space that puts outdoor living at the centre. Sea views are a natural feature of most homes.

Indigo is focused on a high quality of life for its residents: a modern gym, a social club and a generous pool with garden form the beating heart of the complex. Underground parking and storage are included as standard.

Location

Estepona, the New Golden Mile

Zenity Indigo sits on Estepona's New Golden Mile — the coastal strip connecting Estepona with Marbella, known for its luxury resorts, wide beaches and excellent golf facilities. The beach is within walking distance, and Estepona's vibrant town centre with its flower-lined streets and restaurants is reachable in under 10 minutes. Marbella is 30 minutes away.
