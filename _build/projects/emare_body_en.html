The project

28 villa-apartments
frontline to the sea

Emare is an exclusive complex of 28 spacious villa-apartments and penthouses with 4 or 5 bedrooms, spread over a 2-hectare estate directly on the beach of the New Golden Mile in Estepona, near Laguna Village. The project was delivered in 2019 and combines a timeless, contemporary design with the latest technologies — an architectural benchmark on a coast that already enjoys worldwide fame.

The residence

Spacious surfaces
up to 840 m²

Every home is designed for life, with extra-large sun terraces that form an integral part of the property thanks to their low altitude and continuous floor level. The sea is part of each of these exceptional villa-apartments, with contemporary interior surfaces and impressive windows like in a luxury villa.

The view

Panoramic views,
right on the sea

Thanks to its frontline location, you look directly out over the Mediterranean Sea — from the terrace, the pool or the garden. The heated 37 by 9 metre infinity pool merges with the blue of the ocean and the green garden.
