The project

Sleek elegance
in the pine forests of Elviria Hill

ZEW Elviria is an exclusive new-build project of 88 duplex apartments, set in the tranquil pine forests of Elviria Hill, one of the most sought-after neighbourhoods in East Marbella. This modern residential complex combines sleek elegance with natural tranquillity.

The residence

Two- or three-bedroom apartments — some with an optional fourth room — featuring bright interiors, large terraces and panoramic views over the green landscape and mountains. Minimalist architecture and refined finishes create a timeless atmosphere.
