The project

353 homes,
5 minutes from la Carihuela

Magna Torremolinos is an exclusive new-build project of 353 homes, located on Avenida Carlota Alessandri in Torremolinos, within walking distance (around 5 minutes) of the iconic Playa de la Carihuela beach. The homes, with 1 to 4 bedrooms, are designed with open spaces, plenty of natural light and spacious terraces; the most exclusive penthouses even have their own private pool, jacuzzi and chill-out zone with panoramic views.
