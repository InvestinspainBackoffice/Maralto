Six villas at the highest point of the Golden Mile

Camojan Six is an exceptional project of just six villas, positioned at the absolute highest point of the prestigious gated community Cascada de Camojan — in the heart of Marbella's Golden Mile. Surrounded by lush nature and protected by 24/7 security, these villas offer a combination of absolute privacy, spectacular panoramic views and maximum luxury.

Each of the six villas is individually designed with an eye for detail: from the architecture to the finish, they exude a timeless elegance befitting the most discerning resident. The elevated hillside setting guarantees views of both the Sierra Blanca mountain ridges and the Mediterranean Sea.

Location

Cascada de Camojan, the most exclusive enclave in Marbella

The area offers absolute privacy and tranquillity, while Marbella's town centre, Puerto Banús and the best beaches on the Costa del Sol are just a few minutes away. Málaga international airport is easily reached by motorway.
