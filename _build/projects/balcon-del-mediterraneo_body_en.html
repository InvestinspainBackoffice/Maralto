The project

Luxury living
on the New Golden Mile

Balcón del Mediterráneo is an exclusive residential project of spacious apartments and penthouses on the New Golden Mile in Estepona. With surfaces of 200 to 300 m² and spectacular views of the Mediterranean Sea, these homes combine comfort with a privileged location near Puerto Banús and Marbella.

The homes

Spacious terraces

Every home features large terraces overlooking the Mediterranean Sea. The apartments and penthouses come with high-quality finishes, fully equipped kitchens and double glazing. Penthouses include a private solarium with their own pool.

Location

Balcón del Mediterráneo is located on the New Golden Mile in Estepona, between Marbella and Estepona centre. Just 10 minutes from Puerto Banús with its luxury marina, designer shops and restaurants. Several top golf courses — including Los Flamingos and El Paraiso — are within walking distance.
