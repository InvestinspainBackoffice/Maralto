Exclusive apartments of 200 to 300 m²
on the New Golden Mile, Estepona

Balcón del Mediterráneo is a select project of luxury apartments on the New Golden Mile of Estepona, one of the most sought-after coastal stretches of the Costa del Sol. The homes — with 3, 4 or 5 bedrooms and surface areas between 200 and 300 m² — are designed for those who want to make the most of the Mediterranean climate: large terraces, open living spaces and a high standard of finish.

Each home includes 2 parking spaces — a rarity in this segment. The location provides easy access to the beach, Estepona's marina and the golf courses of the New Golden Mile, while the centre of Marbella is less than 20 minutes' drive away.

Location

New Golden Mile, Estepona
between beach and Marbella

The New Golden Mile is the coastal zone between Estepona and San Pedro de Alcántara, set between the mountains and the sea. The project is within walking distance of the beach and offers easy access to the A-7 motorway towards Marbella (± 20 min) and Gibraltar (± 45 min). Estepona's harbour with its restaurants and shops is approximately 10 minutes away.
