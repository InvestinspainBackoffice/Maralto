Sphere consists of 33 impressive apartments and penthouses with 2, 3 and 4 bedrooms, set in a unique natural setting in Sotogrande. Each home offers panoramic views over the lush green surroundings. Three-sided facades and floor-to-ceiling windows maximise light, creating sun-drenched spaces that bring the outdoors in.

The generous terraces — from 40 m² to 260 m² — and private gardens of up to 155 m² create a constant dialogue between inside and outside. Sphere is not just a project but a complete lifestyle, surrounded by the nature of Sotogrande and close to the finest golf courses in Europe.

Location

Sotogrande,
where nature, golf and luxury meet

Sotogrande is one of the most exclusive private communities in Europe, renowned for its outstanding golf courses (including Valderrama, Real Club de Golf Sotogrande and La Reserva), polo club, marina and luxurious lifestyle. Located in the province of Cádiz, on the border with Málaga and just 20 minutes from Gibraltar. Estepona is approximately 25 minutes away.
