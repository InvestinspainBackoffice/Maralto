The residence

Spacious terraces,

The homes enjoy an ideal orientation and are characterized by abundant natural light, open living spaces and generous terraces with panoramic views of mountains and water. Interior and exterior flow seamlessly together, with the landscape central to every detail of the design.

Premium Quality

Bosch appliances,
Saloni tiles & Jacob Delafon

Interiors are finished to the highest standards. Think Bosch appliances in fully integrated kitchens with Silestone worktops, designer bathrooms with Saloni tiles and Jacob Delafon sanitary ware. Oak-tone woodwork with sliding cabinet doors and large-format porcelain flooring complete the luxury finish.
