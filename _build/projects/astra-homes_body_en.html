The project

Astra Homes is a modern new-build project comprising 126 apartments with 1, 2 and 3 bedrooms, located in Fuengirola, in the heart of the Costa del Sol. The residence combines a strategic location close to the sea and the city with a quiet, residential setting.

The residence

South-facing terraces,

The homes are designed with a focus on light, space and comfort, and feature spacious south-facing terraces that provide open views and a strong connection between indoors and outdoors. Sizes vary, so both compact and larger family homes are available.
