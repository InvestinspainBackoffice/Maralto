Palm Mar · Tenerife

160 apartments on the southern coast of Tenerife

Palma Real Suites is a new development of 160 apartments and penthouses in Palm Mar, a quiet coastal village at the southernmost tip of Tenerife. Spread across 4 blocks, the project offers 1- and 2-bedroom homes surrounded by tropical gardens with a botanical atmosphere. The heated communal pool, lush planting and proximity to the sea make this an ideal place for both permanent living and investment.

Palm Mar itself is a small and peaceful village, a stone's throw from Las Galletas and Playa de la Tejita — the perfect base for exploring the south of Tenerife.

Location

Palm Mar, the peaceful south of Tenerife

Palm Mar is one of the most understated and attractive villages on the southern coast of Tenerife. No mass tourism — just an authentic atmosphere, crystal-clear water and a pleasant micro-climate all year round. The village is close to Las Galletas, Playa de la Tejita and the Los Palos golf complex. Tenerife South Airport is less than 20 minutes away.
