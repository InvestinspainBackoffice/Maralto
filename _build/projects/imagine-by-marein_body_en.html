The project

15 exclusive villas
in Bel Air

Imagine by Marein is a new development of 15 exclusive detached villas in Bel Air, Estepona. The architecture blends classical lines with contemporary standards, seamlessly integrated into the surroundings. Architects, interior designers, landscape architects and engineers from Marbella collaborated on every detail.

The homes

Private pool, solarium
& spacious basement

Each villa features a private pool of approximately 56 m², a large covered terrace at ground level, a solarium and a garden. The basement can be configured as a fitness or game room. The fully equipped kitchen and high ceilings in the living room create an open and light atmosphere.

Amenities

Sea & mountain views
& premium finishes

The villas offer views of both the sea and the mountains. Every home comes with air conditioning, alarm system and two covered parking spaces. Carefully selected materials and finishes give each room a unique character.
