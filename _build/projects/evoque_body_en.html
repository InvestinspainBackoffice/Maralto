The homes

Generous terraces
& an optional private jacuzzi

The homes are spacious, with bright interiors and generous terraces that connect indoor and outdoor living seamlessly. Large windows and an open layout create a sense of freedom and calm. Penthouses and ground-floor homes offer extra luxury, including the option of a private jacuzzi.

Amenities

Wellness centre
& a gourmet lounge

EVOQUE offers a complete package of amenities that contribute to a comfortable lifestyle. Residents can enjoy swimming pools, a wellness centre with gym, sauna and hammam, and a stylish gourmet lounge for socialising and relaxation. All apartments come with underground parking with charging points and a private storage room.
