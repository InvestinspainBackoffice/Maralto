Balance Nova is a modern residential project comprising 142 apartments with 2 and 3 bedrooms, located in Mijas Costa. Thanks to its elevated position, the homes enjoy beautiful views over the Mediterranean and the surrounding nature.

The apartments are designed with a focus on light, space and comfort. Large terraces, open living spaces and modern architecture create a harmonious connection between indoors and outdoors. The kitchens are fully equipped and the homes feature premium finishes and energy-efficient technology.
