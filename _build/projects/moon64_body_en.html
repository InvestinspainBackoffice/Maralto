Architecture

Two crescent-shaped blocks,
& panoramic sea views

The project consists of two elegant, crescent-shaped architectural blocks with apartments and penthouses spread over four levels. The homes feature 2 bathrooms and spacious, bright living areas that flow seamlessly onto large terraces — ideal for enjoying the Mediterranean climate and panoramic views of the sea and surrounding mountains.

Comfort & amenities

Private garden, solarium
& social club

Ground-floor apartments have a private garden and pool, while the penthouses offer a solarium to make the most of outdoor living. All homes come with fully fitted kitchens, energy-efficient climate control and double glazing, with access to underground parking and a storage room. Communal facilities include an impressive infinity pool, landscaped gardens, a gym and a social club.
